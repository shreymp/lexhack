RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement (this "Lease") is entered into and made effective as of the date of the last signature below, by and between Meridian Prairie Holdings LLC, an Illinois limited liability company ("Lessor"), and Jordan A. Kowalski, an individual ("Lessee"). Lessor and Lessee are collectively referred to herein as the "Parties."

Section 1 — Parties

Lessor's address for notice purposes is 1200 North LaSalle Drive, Suite 800, Chicago, Illinois 60610. Lessee's current mailing address prior to occupancy is 88 East Cermak Road, Apt. 2, Chicago, Illinois 60616. Each Party represents that it has the authority to enter into this Lease.

Section 2 — Premises

Lessor hereby leases to Lessee, and Lessee hereby leases from Lessor, the residential dwelling unit located at 4417 South Drexel Boulevard, Unit 3W, Chicago, Illinois 60653 (the "Premises"), together with the non-exclusive use of common areas appurtenant thereto, subject to the terms and conditions set forth below.

Section 3 — Term

(a) The term of this Lease shall commence on November 1, 2026, and shall terminate on October 31, 2027, unless sooner terminated or extended as provided herein.

(b) Should Lessee remain in possession after the expiration of the term without a new written agreement, occupancy shall convert to a month-to-month tenancy on the same terms and conditions, except that either Party may terminate such tenancy as otherwise provided by law.

Section 4 — Rent

Lessee shall pay to Lessor, as rent for the Premises, the sum of Two Thousand One Hundred Dollars ($2,100.00) per month (the "Rent"), payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction, or setoff, at the address designated by Lessor from time to time.

Section 5 — Security Deposit

(a) Upon execution of this Lease, Lessee shall deposit with Lessor a sum equal to one month's Rent as set forth in Section 4 above, as a security deposit (the "Deposit"), to secure Lessee's faithful performance of the covenants of this Lease.

(b) The Deposit, together with all sums held on behalf of Lessee, shall be held at Prairie Trust & Savings Bank, 900 West Adams Street, Chicago, Illinois 60607, in an account maintained separately from Lessor's own funds.

Section 6 — Late Payment Charge

In the event any installment of Rent is not received by Lessor within three (3) days following its due date, Lessee shall pay to Lessor, in addition to the Rent then owing, a late charge of Fifteen Dollars ($15.00) per day for each day the Rent remains unpaid thereafter, continuing for a period not to exceed thirty (30) days, as liquidated damages for the administrative burden occasioned by the delinquency.

Section 7 — Early Payment Discount

A Lessee who tenders the full Rent payment on or before the first day of the month for which it is due shall receive a rent discount of Twenty-Five Dollars ($25.00) applied against the following month's Rent.

Section 8 — Use and Occupancy

The Premises shall be used and occupied solely as a private residential dwelling for Lessee and Lessee's immediate household, and for no other purpose, commercial or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Lessor.

Section 9 — Utilities and Services

Lessee shall be responsible for arranging and paying for all utilities serving the Premises, including electricity, gas, and telecommunications services, except that Lessor shall pay for water and refuse removal as building-wide services.

Section 10 — Lessee's Maintenance Obligations

Lessee shall keep the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition, shall not permit waste or nuisance, and shall promptly notify Lessor in writing of any condition requiring repair of which Lessee becomes aware.

Section 11 — Lessor's Maintenance Obligations

Lessor shall maintain the Premises, common areas, and building systems in compliance with applicable building codes and shall make repairs necessary to keep the Premises fit for habitation, except for damage caused by the negligence or misuse of Lessee.

Section 12 — Alterations and Improvements

Lessee shall not make any structural alteration, addition, or improvement to the Premises without Lessor's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for minor, non-structural modifications such as picture hanging.

Section 13 — Assignment and Subletting

Lessee shall not assign this Lease or sublet the Premises, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of Lessor, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

Section 14 — House Rules

Lessor reserves the right to adopt, amend, or repeal from time to time such reasonable rules and regulations governing the use of the Premises and the building as Lessor, in its sole discretion, deems appropriate, and any such amended rule shall become binding upon Lessee immediately upon posting in the building lobby or delivery to Lessee, without further amendment of this Lease.

Section 15 — Pets

No pet of any kind shall be kept on the Premises without the prior written consent of Lessor, which consent, if granted, may be conditioned upon payment of an additional pet deposit and compliance with any pet policy Lessor may adopt.

Section 16 — Parking

One unassigned parking space in the rear lot is included with the Premises at no additional charge, subject to Lessor's reasonable rules regarding its use.

Section 17 — Insurance and Limitation of Liability

Lessee is encouraged to obtain renter's insurance covering Lessee's personal property. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Lessor shall not be liable to Lessee, or to any guest or invitee of Lessee, for any injury, loss, or damage to person or property occurring on or about the Premises or the building, regardless of cause, including any injury or damage arising from Lessor's own negligence or the negligence of Lessor's agents or employees, and Lessee shall indemnify and hold Lessor harmless from any such claim.

Section 18 — Casualty and Damage

If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty through no fault of Lessee, Lessor shall, at its option, either repair the damage within a reasonable time or terminate this Lease upon written notice to Lessee, in which case Rent shall be prorated to the date of the casualty.

Section 19 — Default

Should Lessee fail to pay Rent when due, or otherwise breach a material term of this Lease and fail to cure such breach within the period allowed by law after written notice, Lessor may pursue any remedy available at law or equity, including termination of this Lease and recovery of possession.

Section 20 — Confession of Judgment

Lessee hereby irrevocably authorizes and empowers any attorney of any court of record within the State of Illinois to appear for Lessee in any action brought against Lessee arising out of this Lease, to waive service of process, to confess judgment against Lessee for any sums claimed to be due hereunder, together with costs and reasonable attorney's fees, and to waive and release all errors in such proceedings.

Section 21 — Attorneys' Fees

Should Lessor engage counsel to enforce any provision of this Lease or to collect any sum due hereunder, whether or not suit is filed, Lessee shall reimburse Lessor for all reasonable attorneys' fees and costs so incurred, it being understood that this obligation runs from Lessee to Lessor only.

Section 22 — Waiver of Jury Trial

Lessor and Lessee each knowingly, voluntarily, and intentionally waive any right either may have to a trial by jury in any action, proceeding, or counterclaim arising out of or relating to this Lease or the tenancy created hereby.

Section 23 — Court Costs

In the event either Party is required to institute legal proceedings to enforce the terms of this Lease, the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover court costs from the non-prevailing Party as provided by law.

Section 24 — Right of Entry

Except in cases of genuine emergency, Lessor shall provide Lessee no less than forty-eight (48) hours' advance notice, whether written or verbal, before entering the Premises for the purpose of inspection, repair, or showing the unit to prospective tenants, purchasers, or lenders, and any such entry shall occur at a reasonable hour.

Section 25 — Return of Security Deposit

Within sixty (60) days after Lessee vacates the Premises and surrenders possession to Lessor, Lessor shall return the Deposit, or the balance thereof after deducting unpaid rent and the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by Lessee beyond ordinary wear and tear, together with an itemized statement of any such deductions.

Section 26 — Move-Out Condition

Upon surrender of the Premises, Lessee shall, at Lessee's sole cost and expense, engage a licensed professional carpet cleaning service to clean all carpeted floor areas of the Premises prior to vacating, regardless of the condition of such carpeting at the commencement of the tenancy, and shall provide Lessor with a receipt evidencing such service.

Section 27 — Pest Control

Lessee shall be solely responsible, at Lessee's own expense, for the cost of pest control services for the Premises throughout the term of this Lease, regardless of the origin, source, or cause of any insect or rodent infestation.

Section 28 — Notices

Any notice required or permitted under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be deemed delivered when personally delivered or when deposited in the United States mail, postage prepaid, addressed to the Party's address set forth in Section 1 or the Premises, as applicable.

Section 29 — Governing Law and Ordinance Summary

This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois and the ordinances of the City of Chicago. A copy of the City of Chicago Residential Landlord and Tenant Ordinance Summary is attached to this Lease.

Section 30 — Entire Agreement

This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the Premises and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements, whether written or oral. This Lease may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties, except as otherwise expressly provided in Section 14.
