RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES. This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of September 1, 2026, by and between Maple Row Test Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Example ("Tenant"), collectively the "Parties."

2. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the residential unit located at 4127 W. Foster Avenue, Unit 2, Chicago, Illinois 60630 (the "Premises"), together with the fixtures and appliances currently installed in the Premises.

3. TERM. The term of this Agreement shall begin on October 1, 2026, and end on September 30, 2027 (the "Term"), unless sooner terminated as otherwise provided in this Agreement.

4. RENT. Tenant shall pay Landlord rent in the amount of $1,500.00 per month, due in advance on the 1st day of each month, payable to Maple Row Test Properties LLC at the address set forth in the Notices section of this Agreement.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall pay Landlord a security deposit in the amount of $1,500.00 to secure Tenant's performance of Tenant's obligations under this Agreement.

6. LATE CHARGES. If Landlord does not receive the full monthly rent payment by the 5th day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $100.00 in addition to the rent otherwise due for that month.

7. EARLY PAYMENT DISCOUNT. If Tenant pays the full month's rent in cleared funds on or before the 1st day of the month, Landlord shall credit Tenant a $25.00 discount against the following month's rent.

8. USE OF PREMISES. The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence for Tenant and Tenant's immediate household, and for no other purpose, including any commercial or business use, without Landlord's prior written consent.

9. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for arranging and paying for electricity, natural gas, and internet service to the Premises. Landlord shall be responsible for water and sewer service.

10. PETS. Tenant may keep one dog or cat in the Premises, subject to a combined pet weight limit of 40 pounds and Landlord's prior written approval. No more than two pets total are permitted in the Premises at any time.

11. SMOKING. Smoking of any kind, including tobacco and cannabis products, is prohibited inside the Premises and in all common areas of the building.

12. PARKING. Tenant is assigned one uncovered parking space in the rear lot for the Term, at no additional charge. Tenant shall not park in spaces assigned to other units.

13. TRASH AND RECYCLING. Tenant shall deposit household refuse and recycling only in the containers designated for the Premises and located in the rear alley, and shall comply with the City of Chicago's applicable collection schedule.

14. QUIET ENJOYMENT. Landlord covenants that, upon paying rent and performing Tenant's obligations under this Agreement, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly hold and enjoy the Premises during the Term.

15. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Landlord shall maintain the Premises in compliance with applicable building and housing codes and shall make necessary repairs to keep the Premises in a fit and habitable condition, except for repairs required because of Tenant's misuse or neglect.

16. TENANT-RESPONSIBLE REPAIRS. Notwithstanding Section 15, Tenant shall be responsible for the cost of any repair to the Premises of $250.00 or less, regardless of the cause of the damage or the condition requiring repair.

17. GUESTS. Tenant shall not permit any guest to remain overnight in the Premises for more than three (3) nights in any calendar month without Landlord's prior written consent.

18. ADMINISTRATIVE FEE. Tenant shall pay Landlord a one-time administrative fee of $400.00 upon signing this Agreement. This administrative fee is non-refundable under any circumstances.

19. RENEWAL. This Agreement shall automatically renew for a successive term of one (1) year on the same terms and conditions unless Tenant provides Landlord written notice of intent not to renew at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

20. RIGHT OF ENTRY. Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at any time and for any purpose, including routine inspection, maintenance, or to show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers, without providing Tenant advance notice.

21. EMERGENCY ENTRY. Notwithstanding Section 20, if an emergency threatens life or property, Landlord may enter the Premises without advance notice and shall provide Tenant notice describing the entry within two (2) days afterward.

22. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not assign this Agreement or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

23. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Any approved alteration becomes part of the Premises upon installation.

24. INSURANCE. Tenant is encouraged, but not required, to obtain renter's insurance covering Tenant's personal property. Landlord's insurance does not cover loss of, or damage to, Tenant's personal belongings.

25. LIMITATION OF LANDLORD LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury to Tenant or damage to Tenant's property occurring in or about the Premises, even if such injury or damage is caused by the negligence of Landlord or Landlord's agents or employees.

26. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES. If Tenant fails to pay rent when due or otherwise breaches a material term of this Agreement, Landlord may pursue any remedy available under applicable law, including termination of the tenancy upon proper written notice as required by law.

27. ATTORNEY'S FEES. If Landlord brings an action against Tenant arising out of this Agreement, Tenant shall pay all of Landlord's reasonable attorney's fees and court costs incurred in that action.

28. WAIVER OF JURY TRIAL. TENANT AND LANDLORD EACH KNOWINGLY, VOLUNTARILY, AND INTENTIONALLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

29. CONFESSION OF JUDGMENT. Tenant authorizes any attorney to appear on Tenant's behalf in any court of record and confess judgment against Tenant for all sums due under this Agreement, together with costs and reasonable attorney's fees, and to waive and release all errors and rights of appeal.

30. WAIVER OF NOTICE TO QUIT. Tenant waives any right to receive a demand for rent or a notice to quit before Landlord commences an eviction or forcible entry and detainer action against Tenant.

31. TERMINATION RIGHTS. Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving Tenant thirty (30) days' written notice. Tenant may not terminate this Agreement before the end of the Term for any reason.

32. WAIVER OF ORDINANCE RIGHTS. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Tenant waives any and all rights, remedies, and protections available to Tenant under any municipal landlord-tenant ordinance, including the Chicago Residential Landlord and Tenant Ordinance.

33. RETURN OF SECURITY DEPOSIT. Landlord shall return Tenant's security deposit, less any deductions for normal wear and tear, carpet cleaning, and unpaid rent, within sixty (60) days after Tenant vacates the Premises.

34. NOTICES. Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally or by first-class mail to Landlord at 4127 W. Foster Avenue, Chicago, Illinois 60630, Attn: Property Manager, or to Tenant at the Premises.

35. SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions of this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.

36. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements between them, whether written or oral.

37. SIGNATURES. IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.

Landlord: Maple Row Test Properties LLC, by its authorized agent

Tenant: Jordan Example
